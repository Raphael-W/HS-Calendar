How to subscribe

  1. iPhone / iPad: Calendars → Add Calendar → Add Subscription Calendar.
  2. Google Calendar: Other calendars → + → From URL.
  3. Outlook: Add calendar → Subscribe from web.

Keep this link private

Treat the URL like a password — anyone who has it can see your shifts.

If it is shared by accident, it is read-only: it cannot change your shifts, reveal your personal details, or sign in to the High Society app.

If you think someone has your link, email contact@raphaelwreford.com, and it will be revoked.

Your password is never stored — it is encrypted into your personal link so shifts can be refreshed.
